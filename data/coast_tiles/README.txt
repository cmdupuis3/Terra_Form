Coastline tiles derived from OpenStreetMap land polygons
=========================================================

Source : osmdata.openstreetmap.de land-polygons-split-4326  (https://osmdata.openstreetmap.de/data/land-polygons.html)
Built  : 2026-10-02  with scripts/build_coast_tiles.py (clip, union, simplify, quantize, gzip)
