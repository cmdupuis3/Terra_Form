Base Terrain

Moisture picks grass / plains / desert (steppe on brightness, iron-red arid soil on hue); bright white is read as salt or snow by the surrounding non-white climate (arid→salt, cold→snow). Temperature (Köppen zone, altitude, colour) plus a boreal cold tilt (latitude + tundra/taiga colour) and an alpine snow-cover tilt swap in tundra / snow.

Features

Forest & jungle from climate, relief & colour (potential/pre-industrial, not present cover); flood plains on flat desert along rivers (score = colour contrast + discharge + ~20% random); oasis in wet desert spots. Re-roll varies the seeded placement.
